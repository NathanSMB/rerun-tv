The interaction spec

  • Hover any row and the mono show titles yield to ▶ (tune straight in) and ✎ (unfold the editor). No selection step; rows never change height.
  • Keyboard is a full path, not a fallback: Enter tunes the highlighted row, E unfolds it, Esc closes the fold. Hover never gates anything.
  • One fold at a time. Opening a second channel closes the first — ✎ on another row moves the fold there (try it while one is open).
  • Delete is a two-step: the button arms an inline confirm in place — “Delete for good” or “Keep” — no native dialog, no modal. In the mockup the row really disappears; reload to restore.

What gets built

  • Remove the Channels tab from the app bar and retire the standalone ChannelEditor screen; its mutations move into the fold.
  • Remove the Guide's preview aside; the fold-bar carries the on-deck context instead.
  • New: hover controls on rows, the fold-out shell, dense per-show strips (mode · weight · remove, scheduler state beneath, season overrides one level deeper).
  • Rename/renumber become the click-to-edit pills at the fold's top-left, with Delete channel and its inline confirm beside them.
  • + New channel creates the channel and unfolds it immediately.
