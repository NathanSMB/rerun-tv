Rerun TV

A media player for Linux · Electron + ffmpeg

Your library, running like it's Saturday, 1994.

Rerun TV turns a local media library into lean-back TV channels. You build the lineup, the channel decides what's on — shows play in order or on shuffle, multipart stories air start-to-finish, and the next episode starts on its own. Nothing to configure in a text file.

Requires ffmpeg on PATH — it is invoked as a separate program, never bundled.
After downloading: chmod +x rerun-tv-*.AppImage and run it.
Built and tested on Arch (CachyOS) with KDE/Wayland; other distributions should work but are less exercised.

How a channel runs

Scan
Point it at ~/TV. Filenames are parsed, every file is probed once, and anything that doesn't parse waits in an Unmatched queue instead of disappearing.
Schedule
Each pick draws a show by weight, in order or shuffled — down to individual seasons — and a multipart arc is exactly as likely to air as a single episode, then plays uninterrupted.
Play
A local ffmpeg pipeline picks the cheapest path that works, decided at scan time, so tuning in is instant — and the next episode is buffered before the current one ends, so handoffs cut rather than pause.
Sleep
Press S for a dial that runs from off to five hours. When it expires, the episode — or the whole arc — plays to its end, and only then does the channel sign off to black.
